## What I do

I ship slot games. End-to-end — from concept to certification.

My job is to make sure the right things get built, in the right order, by the right people, on time. That means coordinating across design, art, development, QA, math, audio, and external certification bodies — and keeping everyone unblocked while doing it.

I've been in iGaming long enough to know that the hard part is never the game idea. It's the 200 decisions between kickoff and release.
