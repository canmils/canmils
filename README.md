# Joan Casellas

**Senior Game Producer · Product Owner · Delivery Lead**

I lead cross-functional teams from product scope through development, certification and release. At Play’n GO, I managed 6–8 concurrent game productions, supported around 15 commercial releases a year and led an external studio partnership covering scope, budget, delivery and quality. I helped bring reskin production cycles from about 18 weeks down to 10–12 weeks.

I build personal prototypes to explore practical uses of AI in production, from reviewing release evidence to testing a game concept in the browser.
